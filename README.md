## Hi, I'm Trilleo

CS/AI student at Harbin Institute of Technology. I write Minecraft plugins, mods and
datapacks, as well as doing academic projects related to AI and IT.

---

### What I'm building

**[Hex](https://github.com/Trilleo/Hex)**  
A Minecraft QoL utility mod for Hypixel Skyblock.

**[TriSurvival](https://github.com/Trilleo/TriSurvival)**  
A Minecraft RPG-style progression plugin — skills, collections, gear, and an economy —
inspired by Skyblock's progression loop but built for a vanilla survival
world.

**[TriBingo](https://github.com/Trilleo/TriBingo)**  
A Minecraft Bingo plugin with custom goals and innovative gameplay.

**[TriHunt](https://github.com/Trilleo/TriHunt)**  
A modern Minecraft Manhunt plugin with ultimate modes and a variety of features.

**[LILAC_APP](https://github.com/Trilleo/LILAC_APP)**  
A Discord Management bot for LILAC Puzzle server.
